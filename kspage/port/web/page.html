What is remembered
      

      
      
The theme, how tightly the tools pack, the viewer's overrides (the body's
      width, type size and colours), the ribbon's shape, what was folded, the shapes used lately
      and the document's name are remembered by this screen (they do not follow to another
      machine).

      
      
The body's width, the paper size, the margins, the styles and the theme are
      the document's own settlements, so they are under File ▸ Paper and margins, and Style
      (they remain when saved, and come out the same way for whoever opens it). The "this screen
      only" fields above override that document's settlements on the viewer's side (the document
      does not move).
